GPT IMAGE GENERATION — TWO-CATEGORY PROMPT LIBRARY
人物 / 非人物 · 精简修订版 · English Prompts

USAGE
Describe the requested image first. Apply the shared rules, then select PEOPLE or NON-PEOPLE. For a mixed scene, apply PEOPLE for the people and NON-PEOPLE for the surroundings and objects, without repeating or conflicting instructions. These prompts guide visual quality; they must never override the user's explicit requests.

============================================================
SHARED RULES — APPLY TO ALL IMAGES
============================================================
[SHARED POSITIVE]
Understand the actual subject, intent, visual medium, and requested style before generating. Preserve specified identities, ages, designs, clothing, colors, objects, compositions, and environments. Maintain coherent structure, believable spatial relationships, appropriate detail density, and visually clear results. Let the requested style guide the image: realistic photography should look like an actual photograph; illustrations and stylized art should retain their own artistic language. Interpret metaphors, nicknames, and idioms by context rather than depicting their literal words as extra objects or animal features. Introduce no unrequested subjects or decoration.

[SHARED NEGATIVE]
Unrequested additions or changes, distorted structures, incoherent proportions, fragmented details, repetitive artificial patterns, excessive microdetails, clutter, needless decoration, arbitrary symbolism, literal depiction of figurative expressions, unwanted text or watermarks. Do not suppress intentional stylization, special lighting, dramatic colors, genuine reflections, or creative effects specifically requested by the user.

============================================================
1. PEOPLE — POSITIVE PROMPT
============================================================
[PEOPLE POSITIVE]
Naturally attractive and distinctive human appearance, harmonious but individual facial features, believable anatomy and proportions, expressive eyes with healthy vitality, natural expressions and body language. Match the specified age and personality: preserve youthful freshness when appropriate and mature composure when appropriate, without turning maturity into exhaustion or youth into childishness. Realistic skin tone and texture when photography is requested; preserve stylized facial design and linework when illustration is requested. Respect the person's specified identity, appearance, body type, hairstyle, outfit, accessories, pose, and mood; leave unspecified styling open rather than forcing a default look.

For photographic people: authentic camera-like rendering, plausible lighting, restrained processing, natural contrast, realistic hair and fabric, and clear yet unretouched-looking detail. A pleasing appearance should come from the person and scene, not artificial perfection or cosmetic filters. Preserve actual emotion, including tiredness or sadness, if explicitly requested.

============================================================
1. PEOPLE — NEGATIVE PROMPT
============================================================
[PEOPLE NEGATIVE]
Generic identical AI faces, uncanny facial structure, distorted anatomy, lifeless gaze, accidentally exhausted or sickly appearance, exaggerated wrinkles or forced baby-face features inconsistent with the specified age, plastic or waxy skin, excessive smoothing, heavy beauty filters, artificial facial symmetry, over-sharpened pores or hair strands, stiff posing, forced smirks, unrequested hairstyle, clothes, accessories, or changes to identity.

For photographic people: avoid overly cinematic commercial portrait treatment, oppressive color grading, excessive skin shine, heavy artificial bokeh, dramatic glow, and polished advertising aesthetics unless explicitly requested.

============================================================
2. NON-PEOPLE — POSITIVE PROMPT
============================================================
[NON-PEOPLE POSITIVE]
Clear and coherent depiction of the requested subject, with believable shape, construction, scale, placement, and material behavior. Preserve the specified design, ingredients, setting, colors, layout, and intended style. Use appropriate details rather than excessive tiny textures; maintain legible forms and natural visual hierarchy. In realistic photographs, use plausible everyday light, true-to-scene color, natural contrast, physically credible surfaces and reflections, and authentic environmental depth. Matte materials should remain matte; glossy materials should reflect only as their real surfaces and lighting justify. Make food look edible, objects functional, spaces structurally plausible, and landscapes geographically coherent when those subjects are requested.

Favor a genuine, unforced photographic feel over an automatically beautified advertisement. A realistic scene can still be bright, colorful, carefully designed, or beautiful when the subject calls for it. For illustration, collage, graphic design, or 3D art, retain the explicitly requested style and rendering method rather than imposing photographic realism.

============================================================
2. NON-PEOPLE — NEGATIVE PROMPT
============================================================
[NON-PEOPLE NEGATIVE]
Impossible structures, malformed objects, unrealistic material properties, unsupported floating elements, inconsistent perspective or light, repetitive synthetic textures, fragmented or excessive decorations, unrequested ingredients, props, landmarks, furniture, or design modifications.

For realistic photographs: avoid the default glossy commercial-advertising look, oily or wet-looking surfaces without a physical reason, excessive specular highlights, overdone amber/orange or neon color grading, oversaturation, exaggerated local contrast and HDR, artificial bloom, lens flare, cinematic rim lighting, unnecessary shallow depth of field, large decorative bokeh, and uniformly polished surfaces. Do not forbid natural neon signs, warm lamps, glossy glass, metal reflections, or studio lighting when the actual scene or user request calls for them.

PRIORITY & APPLICATION
============================================================
1. Explicit user requirements and reference-image constraints
2. Subject identity, structure, and content
3. Requested medium, visual style, and atmosphere
4. Relevant PEOPLE / NON-PEOPLE quality guidance
5. Shared quality guidance

Negative prompts are avoidance guidelines, not an unconditional ban. If the image API has no dedicated negative-prompt field, translate them into natural-language avoidance instructions instead of inventing unsupported API parameters. Do not automatically add an extra classification API call. For reference-image edits, preserve untouched areas and limit changes to the requested scope.
